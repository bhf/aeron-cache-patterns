Routing is by the request's ENDPOINT PREFIX, not by cache id and not round-robin — write/command
traffic and streaming/hydration traffic land on separate gateways. No server-snippet is used (the
prefixes are disjoint and WebSocket is upgraded natively), so the controller needs no allow-snippets.

Reach the front door by port-forwarding the ingress-nginx controller:

  kubectl port-forward -n ingress-nginx svc/ingress-nginx-controller 8080:80

  # hydrate   (stream):  ws://localhost:8080{{ .Values.stream.path }}/hydrate/<id>
