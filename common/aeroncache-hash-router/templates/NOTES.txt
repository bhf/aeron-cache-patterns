Reach the front door by port-forwarding the ingress-nginx controller, then address caches by id:

  kubectl port-forward -n ingress-nginx svc/ingress-nginx-controller 8080:80

Reads and writes for the same id hash to the same shard (one backend). The chosen shard comes back in
the X-Aeroncache-Shard header (visible with `curl -i` on the http/sse paths).
