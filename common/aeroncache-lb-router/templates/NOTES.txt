Every request is spread across the whole pool — the dual of aeroncache-hash-router, which pins a
cache id to one owning shard. Because every pod is warm with the same caches, any pod can serve any
hydrating subscription, so no session stickiness is needed.

Reach the front door by port-forwarding the ingress-nginx controller:

  kubectl port-forward -n ingress-nginx svc/ingress-nginx-controller 8080:80
  # hydrate (ws): ws://localhost:8080/api/ws/v1/cache/hydrate/<id>  -> round-robined to a warm pool pod

Successive hydrating subscriptions land on different pods; each serves its snapshot from its own near
cache.
